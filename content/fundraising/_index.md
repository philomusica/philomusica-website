      The next Supper Concert will take place on May 23 when we shall be entertained by Robin Nishimura, an outstanding young flautist, Chloe Dunwell, a very fine mezzo-soprano, who is studying at the Royal Welsh College of Music and Drama, and our own James Quinn, whose excellent piano-playing needs no introduction to Philo members.
 
      The other two concerts in the 2026 series will take place on 26 September and 28 November. Book early to avoid disappointment!

      Supper concerts will be given in the usual venue, York Barn, Tredington,  GL20 7BP, about 3 miles from Tewkesbury. As you’ll know if you’ve attended one of these evening before, it is a beautiful venue and there is plenty of parking. Tickets are £10, which includes the concert, supper and a glass of wine. These concerts are great fun and all proceeds go to Philo funds.

      Early booking is strongly advised for both of these events. You can request tickets through our [contact page](/contact).
