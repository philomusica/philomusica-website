  - title: "Sixtieth Anniversary Success"
    body: |
      On 31 October the Philomusica Soloists, Chorus and Orchestra, conducted by Linda Parsons, gave our 60th anniversary concert in the magnificent surroundings of Tewkesbury Abbey

      We presented a programme of music by Elgar, Parry, Stanford and Vaughan Williams; the music choices recognised that British music has always been at the heart of our repertoire.

      We performed to a large and very appreciative audience. Afterwards we received many very complimentary comments. All that positive feedback set the seal on a memorable evening in Philomusica's history.

      We now embark on our 61st year of music-making and we're looking forward to our next concert, a performance of Handel's Messiah in Pershore Abbey on Saturday 20 February 2027. Do, please, come and hear us perform this classic of the choral repertoire.

Philomusica of Gloucestershire & Worcestershire, widely known as Philo, is a very friendly mixed adult choir of around 75 voices. We have group rehearsals in Worcester on Thursdays and Gloucester on Fridays. Whatever your age or musical background, why not come and meet us? You'll be made very welcome!
